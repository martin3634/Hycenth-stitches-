Payment Request

Send when the chat is finished

Fill in the amount and copy this into the WhatsApp chat. It tells the customer to pay and to send the receipt to your DM.

Fire it with one tap: in WhatsApp Business go to Settings → Business tools → Quick replies, save this message as a shortcut such as /pay. While you are chatting, typing /pay then pressing send drops the whole payment request with your account number into the chat.

Measurement Request

You ask for sizes in the chat

Copy this and send it whenever a customer needs to give you their measurements for the fit.
